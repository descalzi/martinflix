# Product: MartinFlix

MartinFlix is a personal media system and its control room. The primary user is the administrator at home, checking whether services are working, finding problems and eventually managing backups and services. Household members may later browse and request media with fewer permissions. The first surface is a local, read-only operating console; it must clearly distinguish implemented capabilities from plans. The source of truth for visual guidance is the sibling uiux design library. No brand assets are provided.
